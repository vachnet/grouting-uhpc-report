Executive answer

World: a niche worth about THB 16–30 billion a year.

In full: A niche worth roughly THB 16–30 billion a year worldwide; products clearly reach 100 MPa, and use cases are strong for only of applications.

Strength above 100 MPa is demanded by wind-turbine grout, UHPC bridge-deck joints and repair overlays. Most other uses are lab studies or have no evidence yet.

Where to look: the world opportunity map in section 3e scores countries; Asia-Pacific detail is in section 3d.
So what: the buyers are specification-driven (turbine makers, offshore certifiers, highway departments), so approval lists matter more than price. Three groups hold almost all evidence: wind, bridges, repair. of applications have only lab evidence or none, so treat them as options, not markets.
Read with care: market sizes come from paywalled press-release teasers that disagree widely (THB 16–61bn for UHPC alone; published USD 0.5–1.8bn). Application and regional TAM, SAM and SOM are our estimates with stated assumptions, trust Low. Region market figures are not published for ≥100 MPa grout, only for wider grout or UHPC.

Data tags: FACT figure on a page we opened · VENDOR CLAIM maker's own data sheet · QUOTE trade or report-seller statement, often a teaser · ESTIMATE our calculation or assumption. Every chart carries its links directly under it. MPa = megapascal; 1 N/mm² = 1 MPa; 1,000 psi = 6.895 MPa.

3b · Wind-turbine projection

World wind installs are forecast to rise from 165 GW in 2025 to about 194 GW a year in 2026–2030.

In full: World wind installs are forecast at about GW a year in 2026–2030 against GW in 2025, with China about 63% of 2026 installs and Europe and the USA forecast to grow faster (about ×1.5).

Wind is the largest source of ≥100 MPa grout demand. Grout per turbine ranges from under 1 t (onshore, trade article) to 150 t (offshore jacket), so offshore projects, not turbine counts, drive tonnes.

3c · Precast bridge-deck joints (field-cast UHPC)

USA and Canada: UHPC deck joints are proven; no published count in China, Europe or the rest of Asia-Pacific.

In full: Field-cast UHPC joints between precast deck panels are proven in the USA and Canada (over 400 US bridges have used UHPC, mostly field-cast connections; specs 120–152 MPa) but have no published count in China, Europe or the rest of Asia-Pacific.

The joint is a narrow gap (about 150–200 mm wide) between precast deck panels filled with pourable UHPC so the deck behaves as one slab. Volumes are small per bridge, so the money is in price per m³, not tonnes.

    Appendix

    Method, assumptions and what is not verified

    Method and limits

    • Scope: cement-based grout, UHPC premix and pourable repair mortar with a stated 28-day compressive strength of at least 100 MPa. Epoxy and polyurethane grouts are out of scope. Steam-cured Japanese Ductal and unverified claims are listed but not counted.
    • Research: source rows from round 1 (four product scouts, one use-case researcher, one market-size researcher) and round 2 (wind projection, bridge-deck joints, second-source check of use cases, regional parent markets); built-in web search only (Exa was not authorised). Evidence file: uhpc-grout_evidence_global_2026-10-27.csv; notes: uhpc-grout_notes_global_2026-10-27.md.
    • Estimation method: TAM from installs × tonnes × price (wind) or regional UHPC × segment share; SAM = TAM × share needing ≥100 MPa × status multiplier; SOM = SAM × open share; low, base and high cases (section 3). The percentages are ours, not published. Round 1 used global TAMs and gave SAM THB 14.9bn (USD 451M); the regional model replaces it.
    • Currency: every number is shown in THB at 33 per USD (planning rate); AUD at 0.65 USD (assumption). Published USD figures stay in the evidence file and in the verify table as "published USD". Converted figures are ESTIMATE.
    • Not verified: every market-size figure is a teaser; DNV-ST-0126, EN 1504-6, CSA S6, DAfStb and JSCE texts were not opened; 26 use-case rows rest on search snippets; products tagged "unverified" rest on search summaries.
    • Blocked or failed sites: Holcim/Amrize Ductal sheets (read via archive copies; live site has none), Mapei Canada product page (404; Mapei China data sheet read in Chrome from screenshots), Kikusui and Sino-Sina (not retried), Sika Japan PDFs (404), Sika Singapore Davco PDF (unreadable), MBCC Asia-Pacific page (522), Bunnings NZ (bot check, not bypassed), Shopee, Lazada and Alibaba (no readable prices), many Chinese seller sites (connection or certificate errors).
    • Earlier work reused: the onshore wind price (THB 43.6/kg, from USD 1.32) and the cross-check of THB 3.2bn from the SikaGrout-9400 report (market_report, section 2); Thai construction chemicals THB 17.1bn (USD 519.44M) from earlier Thailand research (G1-32), not re-opened here.
